Where it stops

What it doesn’t do. Yet, or ever.

It is not fully deterministic.119 rules from published research. 55 have a deterministic detector; the rest are checked by an LLM reviewer. Each finding says how it was verified.
The LLM review reads JS/TS only, for now.Other languages get the deterministic pre-scanner.
It doesn’t compare revisions yet.Every push gets its own review. Failing a check because a revision got worse is on the roadmap.
It won’t catch everything.Nothing blocks 100% of AI vulnerabilities. We document the gaps.
