Where it stops

What it doesn’t do. Yet, or ever.

Not fully deterministic

55 of 119 rules have a deterministic detector. The rest need an LLM, and each finding says how it was verified.

LLM review: JS/TS only, for now

Other languages get the deterministic pre-scanner.

No revision-over-revision gate yet

Every push gets its own review. Failing a check because a push got worse is on the roadmap.

Won’t catch everything

Nothing blocks 100% of AI vulnerabilities. We document the gaps.
